// 03 — Process

Road Map

01
Step 1

Order

We kick things off with a detailed conversation about your vision — brand goals, style references, deliverables, and timeline. Once aligned, the project is formally accepted and we lock in the brief.

⟶ Project Kickoff
02
Step 2

Half Payment

To begin the design work, a 50% advance payment is required. This allows us to fully dedicate our time and creative resources to your project without interruption.

⟶ Payment: 50% Advance
03
Step 3

Samples

Within 10 business days you'll receive initial template samples — the first visual representations of the concept — giving you a strong feel for the direction and space for meaningful feedback.

⟶ 10-Day Delivery Window
04
Step 4

Finalizing

We enter a collaborative editing phase where you provide feedback and we refine every detail — colors, typography, layout, and composition — until everything is exactly how you envision it.

⟶ Unlimited Revisions Phase
05
Step 5

Completion

Once satisfied with the final designs, the remaining 50% is due. After confirmation, all final files are delivered in print-ready and digital formats — ready to launch.

⟶ Payment: 50% Balance + File Delivery
